Support

We typically respond within 1 to 2 business days.

Email us: support@wanderwork.io

For questions, bug reports, billing issues, or anything else related to Wander/Work or the browser extension.

Extension Help

  • Can't connect: Make sure you have a Pro or Premium account and are using the correct extension key from your Settings page at wanderwork.io.
  • Autofill not working: The extension supports Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Workable, and Jobvite. Other job boards are not yet supported.
  • Key not found: Log in to wanderwork.io, go to Settings, and copy your extension key from the Extension section.
  • Documents not arriving: Check your spam folder. Documents are sent to the email on your Wander/Work account and typically arrive within 2 minutes.

Account Help

  • Forgot password: Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page.
  • Cancel or change plan: Go to Settings > Payment in the app, or email us.
  • Delete account: Go to Settings > Account and select Delete Account, or email us at support@wanderwork.io.

Report a Bug

Found something broken? Email support@wanderwork.io with a description of what happened, the page or feature it occurred on, and your browser/OS. We'll look into it promptly.
